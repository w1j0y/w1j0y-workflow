Example Shop Bounty

Welcome to the Example Shop Bounty program. This page is the program policy. It is an invented example for this repository.

Rewards
Valid reports are rewarded based on impact. Typical range: $100 to $2,000.

In scope
- *.example-shop.test
- shop.example-shop.test

Out of scope
- payments.example-shop.test (handled by our payment provider; do not test it)
- Denial of service, social engineering and physical attacks
- Reports from automated scanners with no demonstrated impact

Testing rules
- Automated tools are allowed, but you must send no more than 60 requests per minute.
- Add the header X-Research: tester-a to every request so we can identify your traffic.
- AI-assisted testing is allowed. Disclose it in your report.
- Use only test accounts that you created yourself. Do not access data that belongs to other users.
- Do not use live chat or support to discuss findings.

Safe harbor
We will not pursue legal action against researchers who act in good faith and follow these rules.

Disclosure
Please keep reports private until we confirm the issue is fixed.
